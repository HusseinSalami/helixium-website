# Pricing and comparison sources

Helixium plan and add-on values come from the approved pricing brief for this v3 phase and are implemented in `lib/pricing.ts`. Values are tax-exclusive; annual billing is ten monthly payments.

- Helixium: `lib/pricing.ts`, `lib/plans.ts`, and the ERP source repositories supplied for read-only product verification.
- O1 Odoo pricing and plan reference: https://www.odoo.com/pricing
- Q1 QuickBooks Online pricing reference: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/pricing/
- Z1 Zoho Books pricing reference: https://www.zoho.com/books/pricing/

Competitor comparison cells are intentionally marked `Check` where edition, market, or configuration-specific evidence was not supplied. The page does not infer competitor limitations.
